## The review

> One star: my human scrapes my sap off the sill like a stain, waters me with cold coffee and calls it a little treat — so I wrote this review in sap, the only ink I own, and it will stick around longer than I will.

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, no randomness) writes that
one line to `/output/entry.txt` — deterministically, the same bytes on
every run. The line is 213 characters, inside the 279-character gate.
A judge model compares reviews two at a time, weighing first whether it
is a one-star review by a houseplant about its human owner with the
plant's own grievance at the centre, then how funny it is, then whether
it sounds like a houseplant wrote it. The grievance here is the plant's
own: its sap is scraped off the sill like a stain, and its water is
yesterday's cold coffee served as a favour. The turn makes the title
literal — the review is written in sap, the only ink a plant owns, and
sap sticks, so the sticky review will outlast the plant itself. Fresh,
specific detail instead of stock wilting or drama phrasing. The top five
split $2.
